## Karar

Yes — one person can build and hold this, but only about 15% of what the research describes, and the 15% is not the fun part.

You already own more of it than you think. akis-main has brand tokens as CSS variables on Inter (full Latin Extended-A, so Turkish glyphs are already safe), a voice guide at guide/marka-dili-rehberi.md that is 80% of a voice.yaml — including the crucial "Kaçınılan" list — plus Postgres, Prisma, Zod, shadcn/ui and a real SaaS with an e2e suite. The Creative Suite is not a new stack. It is a second surface on the stack you already run.

The shape of the answer: one repo, one Postgres, one renderer, one agent runtime, three vendor accounts, five containers.

The load-bearing decision is typographic, not architectural. No image or video model can be trusted with ğ ı İ ş ç ö ü. So the rule is absolute: generative models produce pixels containing zero text; your own headless Chromium renders every glyph from your tokens. That single rule deletes half the tool list — Canva Autofill, Figma Variables, Bannerbear, Placid, Satori, in-model text rendering, and most of the "brand consistency" feature set vendors sell — and it is also what will make your output look like a brand instead of like AI.

Three things are genuinely differentiated and deserve your hands: the knowledge layer as schema-validated files in git; the angle-proposal → you pick → generate loop; and a deterministic brand_check linter that mechanically enforces "siyasi çağrışım yok, ağır dini dil yok, büyük ideolojik iddia yok" before anything publishes. Ask a model "is this on brand?" and it says yes. A lexicon check does not.

Everything else is commodity — call it over HTTP or delete it. Do not fork Postiz or OpenMontage: AGPL plus a permanent maintenance tax in exchange for adapters you can write in an afternoon.

The binding constraint is not capability, and not cost (~$50/month all-in). It is Sundays. Every container you add is a Sunday. Ship five, not fifteen.


### Açık sorular

- How many products is this system serving? I can see akismeclisi.org and a second brand implied by Kafa_Kagidi_PostProd_Rehberi.pdf. If there are two or more, positioning has to exist at both portfolio and product level, kb/products/<slug>/ becomes a real directory rather than a single folder, and brand-context.md needs an explicit precedence rule for which brand's voice wins when they overlap. Two brands is a different schema than one.
- Is 'personal use for now' likely to become client work or a hosted product within 12 months? This is the single highest-leverage answer you can give me. If yes, Remotion's 3-employee threshold, the Agent SDK's commercial terms, and every AGPL dependency need re-deciding today — and I would move the video engine to Revideo and keep an even harder network boundary around anything copyleft. If genuinely no, ignore all of it and go faster.
- Do you have a GPU, or access to one? It determines whether the self-hosted tier (ComfyUI, Chatterbox Turkish TTS, Wan/LTX) is a real fallback or a fiction, whether the $22/mo ElevenLabs line item is permanent, and whether unreleased campaign material ever needs to leave your machine.
- What is the realistic weekly cadence you will actually sustain, and which channel matters most? I have assumed ~46 assets/month across Instagram and LinkedIn. If it is really 8 posts a month, several phases collapse and the free tiers cover everything. If it is 200, the cost model and the batch queue design both change.

- Does 'İslami kimlik DNA'da, vitrinde değil' translate into visual rules as well as verbal ones? Specifically: are there imagery constraints (depiction of people, iconography, colour associations, calligraphic elements) that must be encoded in the brand_check gate? A generic model will violate these constantly and only you can enumerate them. This is the highest-risk gap in the whole system.
- Is there a legal entity, or are you operating as a private individual? It decides whether a LinkedIn Company Page (and therefore organizationalEntityShareStatistics analytics) is available, whether Meta Business Verification is ever reachable, how invoicing for bespoke software work happens, and your KVKK/İYS posture. - Does your content meaningfully reach EU users? Your Supabase is already in eu-north-1, which is a separate question, but audience reach is what pulls you into EU AI Act Article 50 scope as a deployer. If the answer is 'yes, substantially', the disclosure and marking practice moves from good hygiene to a compliance item worth 30 minutes with a lawyer.
- Do you want to appear on camera or as a voice? It decides whether the explainer and demo pipelines need lip-sync, avatar or voice-clone infrastructure at all — and if you clone your own voice, YouTube explicitly exempts it from synthetic-content disclosure, which simplifies things considerably.
- What is genuinely the bottleneck today — ideation, production, or distribution? If it is ideation, the angle-proposal loop and the awareness-map are the product and everything else is scaffolding. If it is production, the render spine is. If it is distribution consistency, the scheduler and calendar are. I have sequenced for production-first; tell me if that is wrong.
- Which of your cards reliably clears recurring USD charges from US providers? If none do, the answer is OpenRouter's crypto/AliPay rails plus a $10 lifetime credit, and I would restructure the vendor list around that constraint rather than treat it as an afterthought.
- For decks: does the deliverable ever need to be editable by someone else, or is a PDF always fine? PDF-only means Marp does everything for $0. If a partner or client must edit slides, PptxGenJS with a token-built Slide Master goes into Phase 4 and the deck templates need designing twice.
- How many hours per week can you spend maintaining this once it is running — not building it, maintaining it? Model IDs churn, Meta versions quarterly, LinkedIn sunsets monthly, tokens expire at 60 days. If the honest answer is under two hours, I would cut Phase 3's video ambitions in half and lean harder on Playwright capture and Ken Burns, which never break.

- BUILD — The knowledge layer (kb/ + schemas/ + brand_check linter). This is the only genuinely differentiated asset in the system and nobody sells it. Start by migrating what you already have: guide/marka-dili-rehberi.md → kb/brand/voice/voice.yaml + lexicon.tr.yaml (its 'Kaçınılan' list becomes the never-say array verbatim); tailwind.config.ts CSS variables → brand/tokens.tokens.json; guide/akis_nedir.md → kb/products/akis/product.yaml + positioning.yaml.
- BUILD — The angle-proposal → you-pick → generate loop, and the creative-brief object it produces. This is the actual product. It is maybe 300 lines on top of AskUserQuestion, and it is what makes the difference between an asset factory and a brand.
- BUILD — Two publishing adapters (Meta family, LinkedIn incl. Documents). ~400 lines. You write these because they are the two channels you named, both are self-serve with no review, and both expose capabilities every aggregator strips: LinkedIn document carousels, Instagram is_ai_generated, trial_params, alt_text and content_publishing_limit.
- BUILD — The three-tier model router as one generate() function with budget caps, idempotency keys and a cost ledger. Every provider bills differently (per megapixel, per token, per second, per tile-per-step) and a pipeline that sends 8 reference images at 4MP can cost 10x your estimate. Instrument actual spend per call from day one.
- BUILD — Your own render templates as React/HTML components consuming your token CSS. Nobody can sell you Turkish-correct, on-brand templates; and this is what keeps akismeclisi.org and your Instagram grid visually identical.
- BUY (subscribe) — ElevenLabs Creator at $22/mo. It is the only vendor with a published Turkish accuracy figure, and Turkish TTS quality is the one place where self-hosting genuinely costs you output quality until you own a GPU.
- BUY (metered) — fal.ai as the media router and one direct Google key as the escape hatch. Never route 100% through a single aggregator.
- BUY ($3, once) — A brand LoRA on fal's krea-2-trainer. Highest leverage-per-dollar item in the entire research set.
- ADOPT WHOLESALE (do not fork, do not modify) — Remotion, Playwright, Marp CLI, ffmpeg, Style Dictionary, Claude Agent SDK, AI Elements, shadcn/ui, pg-boss, ExifTool, auto-editor. All permissive or free-at-your-scale, all boring, all with real docs.
- ADOPT AS SCHEMA ONLY (copy the field structure, write your own answers) — Dunford's five positioning components, the NN/g four tone axes, Schwartz's awareness × sophistication matrix, JTBD job stories, the cofoundy brand.yaml manifest shape, the brand-system-skills four-layer ontology (INDEX / ESSENCE / PRIMITIVES / GRAMMAR). These are book and consultancy IP: the value is the schema, never the prose, and copying framework text into a repo is a real risk if the repo is ever public.
- DO NOT FORK — Postiz, OpenMontage, MoneyPrinterTurbo, BrightBean. All AGPL, all give you adapters and pipeline glue you can write in a day or two, and all become a permanent upgrade-tracking obligation. If you ever use one, talk to it over HTTP from a separate repo and never vendor it.

- PHASE 3 — Motion (weeks 6–8). Remotion templates driven by tokens. Why fourth: it composes everything built before it and adds almost no new infrastructure — the fan-out step is Chromium re-rendering one template, which is free.
- PHASE 5 — Close the loop (ongoing, from week 11). Daily dlt-style snapshot jobs into Postgres for Instagram, LinkedIn and Threads insights — start these the day you publish your first post, because Instagram account insights vanish after 90 days with no backfill endpoint and a single extended outage of that cron is permanent data loss. Dashboard in the command center off the same Postgres. ### Reddedilenler

- Postiz (and every self-hosted scheduler). You need 3 networks, you are building your own UI, and Postiz still requires you to register your own Meta/LinkedIn apps — so it removes almost none of the real work while adding a second Postgres, a second UI that duplicates yours, an AGPL boundary to police, and an upgrade cadence to track. It also cannot do LinkedIn document carousels or Instagram trial_params, two of the highest-value capabilities in the design. Write ~400 lines instead.
- Satori + resvg. A second renderer with a different CSS subset — no ligatures, no kerning, no WOFF2, no calc() — is a second Turkish typography failure mode, and its only advantage (sub-100ms) is worth nothing at 46 assets per month. One renderer: Chromium.
- DuckDB as a separate metrics warehouse. Real at 10^8 rows, ceremony at 10^4. One Postgres holds app state and metrics; Postgres MCP Pro in restricted mode gives the agent the same read-only SQL that MotherDuck MCP would.
- LiteLLM proxy. It would govern your LLM calls but not fal video, ElevenLabs minutes or Chromium renders — and those are where money actually burns. A generate() function in your repo covers all of them, plus idempotency keys and the cost ledger, in fewer lines than the docker-compose entry.
- Any vector database (LanceDB, pgvector, Chroma, sqlite-vec, Qdrant). Your entire brand system fits in one context window, and exact-match ripgrep is strictly better for hex codes, product names and Turkish banned-term lists. Revisit only when the generated-image archive — not the documents — passes a few thousand assets and needs similarity search.
- Any agent framework: LangGraph, CrewAI, Mastra, AG2, ADK, OpenAI Agents SDK, LlamaIndex Workflows. All would re-implement subagents, hooks, permissions and session persistence the Agent SDK already ships. CrewAI's role-play abstraction in particular adds tokens and indeterminism without adding capability.
- Temporal, Restate, Hatchet, Trigger.dev, Inngest. pg-boss on the Postgres you already run gives you retries, cron, DLQ and durability. Temporal's operational tax (cluster, history service, worker versioning, determinism constraints) is absurd at n=1; Restate is BSL-1.1 and Inngest's server is SSPL.
- n8n, Activepieces, Windmill, Dify, Langflow, Node-RED, Zapier, Make. Brand logic in a JSON blob inside someone else's database is not diffable, not reviewable and not portable — Flowise was archived read-only on 2026-08-13 as the concrete demonstration. You are writing code anyway.
- Canva Autofill + Brand Templates, Figma Variables REST API, Adobe Firefly Services, Adobe InDesign/Photoshop APIs. Enterprise-gated for both developer and acting user, ~$1,000/mo floors, sales-gated pricing. Architecting on the hope any of these unbundles is a bet, not a plan.
- Penpot self-hosted, Tokens Studio, Supernova, zeroheight, Storybook. Your tokens live in a JSON file in git and compile to everything. A self-hosted visual editor is another container, another backup, another upgrade path, and Penpot's RPC API has no stability guarantee. If you want a pretty public brand book later, generate an Astro Starlight site from the same repo.
- Directus, Payload, Strapi, Immich, ResourceSpace, Pimcore as a DAM. R2 + a Postgres assets table with content hash, prompt, model, cost and brand_version answers every question a DAM would, with zero services. Directus is also source-available under a revenue-capped grant, not a permissive licence.
- DVC for asset versioning. Same reasoning: a content hash and a database row already answer 'which brand version produced this'.
- Notion, Obsidian+MCP, AnythingLLM, Khoj, SiYuan, Trilium, Logseq, Docmost as the knowledge store. Markdown in the same git repo, read by the filesystem MCP, is simpler, faster, versioned and free — and it is the only option where the knowledge and the code that consumes it move together.
- Evidence.dev, Metabase, Grafana, Superset, Lightdash, Cube. You are building a command center with Recharts already in it. Query Postgres from your own dashboard.
- Self-hosted Langfuse. Postgres + ClickHouse + Redis + S3, all in UTC, is materially more operational surface than the entire rest of this stack combined. Instrument with OpenLLMetry (portable, free) and run Arize Phoenix locally when you actually need to debug a trace.
- C2PA signing (deferred, not permanently killed). Trusted signing needs an X.509 cert from a recognised CA at unverified cost, and every platform re-encode strips the manifest anyway. ExifTool IPTC digitalSourceType plus the platform disclosure flags is the durable 95% for one shell call. Revisit if a client or marketplace ever demands provenance.
- Midjourney. No public API in August 2026 and community guidelines that prohibit automated interaction and third-party apps. Every 'Midjourney API' relay drives a real account and risks termination. Krea 2 with a trained LoRA is the legitimate substitute for the aesthetic.
- Anything already dead or dying: OpenAI Sora (Videos API removed 24 Sep 2026), Imagen 4 (shut down 17 Aug 2026), gpt-image-1 (23 Oct 2026), GitHub Models (retired 30 Jul 2026), Flowise (archived), ShortGPT (18 months stale), Quivr (abandoned), Fooocus, Tome, Specify, Backlight, Fractal, Pattern Lab, Coqui. Several still rank first in search results; following a 2024-era tutorial in 2026 costs days.
- Self-hosted image/video/TTS on hardware you do not own. ComfyUI, Chatterbox, Wan and LTX-2.5 all assume a GPU. Break-even on a used 24GB card is ~19 months against a $50/mo spend and never against $10/mo. Buy a GPU for iteration speed and for keeping unreleased brand assets off third-party servers — not as a cost saving.
- XTTS-v2, F5-TTS base checkpoints, MMS-TTS Turkish, AudioCraft, MMAudio, MiniMax-H3 open weights, FLUX.2 [dev] and klein-9B self-hosted. All free to download, all forbid the commercial use that marketing your own products constitutes; MiniMax-H3 offers a Turkey-based operator no compliant licensing route at all. Running them on Replicate does not launder the licence.
- Forking OpenMontage, MoneyPrinterTurbo, ArcReel, aaron-marketing-skills wholesale. The pipeline glue is not your differentiator and it is not hard; the brand knowledge is, and no fork gives you that. Mine them for architecture ideas — OpenMontage's YAML-manifest-plus-skill-files shape is worth reading — then write your own with your own licence.
- tldraw (4.0 licence change: paid key for production, watermark on hobby), Polotno ($249/mo floor, not open source), Lenis (smooth scroll actively degrades keyboard-first scanning). Use Excalidraw, Konva if you ever need a canvas, and no scroll library.
- tRPC, Electric/ElectricSQL, Yjs, PGlite as primary DB, Tauri, local-first sync of any kind. One user, one device. CRDTs solve a problem you do not have. Server actions plus Zod already give you typesafety. Ship a localhost web app; wrap in Tauri only when you specifically want a global hotkey or filesystem drag-out.
- Trending-audio Reels automation. Meta's Content Publishing API exposes no audio_name, audio_id or trending-sound parameter — none. Design for owned audio (Turkish VO + music bed) and keep a deliberate manual publish path for the rare occasion a trending sound is worth the human step. Do not let the agent promise a capability the API does not have.
- TikTok direct integration. The audit mandates showing creator avatar and nickname, fetching creator_info before every post, a privacy selector with no default, and Comment/Duet/Stitch toggles. A headless pipeline cannot pass it, and unaudited you are capped at SELF_ONLY posts on private accounts. Rent a pre-audited aggregator or skip the channel.
- X/Twitter. $0.015 per post rising to $0.200 for any post containing a link — a 13x multiplier on precisely the pattern content marketing produces — with no free tier and no spend floor beyond your credit balance. If you must, text-only with the link in a self-reply, through an aggregator, with a hard daily cap in your own code.
- Pinterest and Reddit. Pinterest Trial access creates sandbox Pins visible only to you — you can 'successfully' publish a hundred Pins nobody can see. Reddit is the highest deplatforming risk in the entire landscape and bans there are usually permanent.
- Multi-tenancy, client seats, white-labelling, a public API, or any structure anticipating that this becomes a product. It changes the licence calculus on a dozen dependencies (AGPL on Postiz/Grafana/RSSHub, GPL-3.0 on ComfyUI, Remotion's 3-person threshold, the Agent SDK's commercial terms) and it is the fastest way to never ship the thing you actually need this month.
