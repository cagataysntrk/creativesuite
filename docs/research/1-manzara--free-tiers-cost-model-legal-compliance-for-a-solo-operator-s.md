## Özet

The economics are dramatically in your favour; the legal calendar is not. On cost: a 46-asset/month pipeline (20 IG, 8 Reels, 12 LinkedIn, 2 explainers, 4 decks) is essentially free on text. Google's Gemini free tier plus Groq's free tier plus Cerebras cover every LLM call you will make, and Cloudflare Workers AI's 10,000-neurons/day free allocation covers ~170 FLUX.1-schnell 1024px images per day at zero cost. Realistic totals: a cheap tier lands at roughly $5-12/month (mostly a VPS), a sane mid tier at ~$50/month, and a full quality tier (Gemini 3.1 Pro text, Nano Banana images, Veo 3.1 Fast video, ElevenLabs Turkish voice) at ~$110-160/month. Video is the only line item that can blow up: Veo 3.1 Standard at $0.40/s turns 8 minutes of footage into $190. GitHub Models — a staple free tier in 2025 — was fully retired on 30 July 2026; delete it from your plans. On legal: EU AI Act Article 50 became applicable on 2 August 2026, twelve days ago, with machine-readable marking for pre-existing systems due 2 December 2026 and fines up to EUR 15M / 3% of turnover. It reaches non-EU operators whose AI output is used in the EU. Separately, YouTube, TikTok and Meta all now run mandatory synthetic-content disclosure with C2PA auto-detection, and Gemini stamps SynthID on every image it makes — so your posts will be auto-labelled whether or not you disclose. The Turkish-specific traps are KVKK cross-border transfer rules (SCCs notifiable within 5 business days), İYS consent registration for any marketing messaging, and the fact that free-tier Gemini prompts are human-reviewed and used for training — never put your brand kit or client data through them.

<details><summary>Notlar</summary>

**Google Gemini API — Free Tier** — CRITICAL: Google's own terms say on the Unpaid Services 'human reviewers may read, annotate and process' your submissions and Google uses them to improve products; 'Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to the Unpaid Services.' So the free tier must never see your brand kit internals, client names, product roadmaps or audience data. Google moved the exact per-model RPM/RPD numbers off the docs page into the AI Studio rate-limit dashboard — check yours at aistudio.google.com/rate-limit. Secondary sources report Dec-2025 quota cuts to roughly 2.5 Pro 5 RPM/100 RPD, 2.5 Flash 10 RPM/250 RPD, Flash-Lite 15 RPM/1000 RPD, 250k TPM shared.

**Gemini image generation (Nano Banana family)** — Every generated image carries a SynthID watermark ('All generated images include a SynthID watermark'). That is a compliance asset under EU AI Act Art. 50(2) — the provider-side machine-readable marking is already done for you — but it also means Instagram/TikTok/YouTube will auto-apply an AI label to your posts regardless of what you declare. Plan your creative around being labelled, not around hiding it.

**Google Veo 3.1** — Arithmetic that matters: 8 Reels x 20s + 2 explainers x 90s = 340s of finished video; with a realistic 40% reject rate that is ~475s of generation. At Standard = $190/month. At Fast 1080p = $57/month. At Lite 720p = $23.75/month. This single choice is the biggest lever in your whole cost model.

**Google Imagen 4** — Flat per-image billing is easier to budget than Nano Banana's token model. SynthID watermarked.

**Groq** — Rate limits apply at the ORGANIZATION level — extra API keys do not multiply quota. Your binding constraint is TPD (100K/day on Llama 3.3 70B), not RPM: a brand-kit-heavy agentic pipeline at 60K input tokens per run exhausts that in under two runs. Keep context lean or route long-context work to Gemini. Adding a card unlocks the free Developer tier with ~10x limits.

**Cerebras Cloud** — 5 RPM on the free tier is genuinely restrictive for parallel agent fan-out. Paid Developer tier removes hourly/daily caps and lifts gpt-oss-120b to 1M TPM / 1K RPM.

**Mistral AI La Plateforme — free 'Experiment' tier** — EU-hosted (France) which simplifies any GDPR/AI Act story if you ever serve EU audiences. Explicitly positioned for evaluation, not production — a ToS risk if you run a daily content pipeline on it.

**Cloudflare Workers AI** — Your whole workload is roughly 300 image generations/month (~17,300 neurons total) against a 300,000/month free allocation. Schedule heavy batches just after the 00:00 UTC reset. Cloudflare also gives you Workers/R2/D1 on the same account, so the scheduler and asset store can live there too.

**Hugging Face Inference Providers** — The widely repeated '100K monthly Inference Provider credits' figure for free users contradicts HF's own $0.10 table — treat it as UNVERIFIED marketing conflation. ZeroGPU Spaces (dynamic H200, ~3.5 min/day free, more on PRO) is a genuinely useful free GPU path for running FLUX or Wan demos, though not for unattended pipelines.

**GitHub Models** — VERIFIED from GitHub's own changelog and docs ('GitHub Models has been fully retired'). Announced 1 July 2026, closed to new customers 16 June 2026, brownouts 16 and 23 July, hard shutdown 30 July 2026. GitHub points users to Azure AI Foundry. If any 2025-era tutorial or template in your repo references GitHub Models, it is dead code.

**OpenRouter** — The $10 lifetime credit purchase that lifts free-model limits from 50 to 1,000 requests/day is the highest-leverage $10 in this entire report. Crypto payment (USDC, 5% fee) is a clean workaround if Turkish card 3-D Secure or FX limits block you elsewhere.

**Together AI** — $0.0027/image means your full 300-image month costs $0.81 — a trivially cheap paid backstop when Cloudflare's daily neuron allocation runs out.

**DeepInfra** — The formula-based image pricing is genuinely useful for a cost model: you can compute exact spend per asset before generating. No free tier at all, so this is a deliberate paid choice, not a freebie.

**Nebius Token Factory (formerly Nebius AI Studio)** — There are no permanently free models — trial credit sits on top of pay-as-you-go. EU hosting is a mild AI Act / GDPR advantage if you ever expand beyond Turkish audiences.

**SambaNova Cloud** — Historically SambaNova ran a free developer tier; as of this check it is not documented on the pricing page. Do not plan capacity around it.

**Fireworks AI** — Note the price increase taking effect 1 September 2026 on all on-demand GPU SKUs — a ~15-30% jump. If you were considering renting a GPU here, that window has closed.

**NVIDIA build.nvidia.com (NIM)** — The strategic value is not the free calls, it is that a NIM you test in the cloud is the same container you can pull down onto your own GPU later. Credits are consumption-capped, not a recurring allowance, so it cannot be a production dependency.

**Pollinations.ai** — Licence is MIT. There are no explicit commercial-use terms in the API docs, which is itself a risk: you have no contractual assurance about output rights or uptime. Never put it in a publishing path — treat it as a sketchpad.

**fal.ai** — The queue API with webhooks is the right shape for a video pipeline: submit, get a job id, get called back. Do not poll. At $0.05/s, 475 seconds of Wan 2.5 output costs $23.75/month — roughly a quarter of Veo 3.1 Fast for the same footage.

**ElevenLabs** — Arithmetic: 8 Reels x ~120 Turkish words + 2 explainers x ~250 words ≈ 10,000 characters; with retakes ~20,000. Starter's 20,000 char cap is exactly at the edge — one bad script rewrite blows it. **Chatterbox Multilingual V3 (Resemble AI)** — IMPORTANT: 'Every audio file generated by Chatterbox includes Resemble AI's Perth (Perceptual Threshold) Watermarker — imperceptible neural watermarks that survive MP3 compression, audio editing, and common manipulations while maintaining nearly 100% detection accuracy.' Your self-hosted TTS is therefore machine-detectably AI, which HELPS your EU AI Act Art. 50(2) position but means you cannot pass it off as human-recorded. Voice-clone only your own voice, with a recorded consent statement kept on file.

**Kokoro-82M** — This is the model most 'free AI stack' listicles recommend, and it is the wrong choice for Turkish content. Use Chatterbox Multilingual instead. Kokoro remains excellent if you produce English-language versions of your explainers.

**FLUX.2 [dev] (Black Forest Labs)** — THE CRITICAL LICENCE NUANCE, quoted from the model card: the weights are under the 'FLUX Non-Commercial License', BUT 'Generated outputs can be used for personal, scientific, and commercial purposes, as described in the FLUX [dev] Non-Commercial License.' **EU AI Act Article 50 — transparency obligations** — VERIFIED dates: Article 50 applies from 2 AUGUST 2026; systems already on the market get until 2 DECEMBER 2026 for the machine-readable marking obligation; content generated and published BEFORE 2 August 2026 does not need retroactive labelling. VERIFIED extraterritorial scope: obligations apply 'irrespective of whether those providers are established or located within the EU or in a third country' and reach non-EU operators 'whose AI outputs are used in Europe' — so a Turkish operator IS in scope for any content reaching EU audiences. Exemptions that matter to you: 'assistive function for standard editing' (spell-check, grammar) does not trigger marking; AI that 'does not substantially alter the input data' is out; evidently artistic/creative/satirical/fictional work needs only 'appropriate' disclosure that does not hamper enjoyment; and AI-generated text escapes the labelling duty when it undergoes 'human review' with 'editorial responsibility' — which your human-in-the-loop approval step provides. **YouTube — Altered or Synthetic Content disclosure** — VERIFIED must-disclose: content that 'makes a real person appear to say or do something they didn't do', 'alters footage of a real event or place', or 'generates a realistic scene that didn't actually occur'.
