## Özet

In 2026 the placement-spec landscape has consolidated around vertical-first. Meta's Ads Guide now recommends 4:5 at 1440x1800 for both Facebook and Instagram feed images, and — notably — recommends 9:16 1080x1920 even for Instagram feed VIDEO. Stories and Reels are 9:16 at 1440x2560 recommended, with an officially documented safe zone of top 14% / bottom 35% / sides 6% (on a 1080x1920 master that is 269px top, 672px bottom, 65px each side, leaving a 950x979 usable band). Aspect tolerance is ±1% on Instagram placements but ±3% on Facebook feed and carousel. LinkedIn is the outlier: 5MB image cap, 1.91:1 1200x628 landscape, 1:1 1200x1200 square, 4:5 720x900 vertical; document ads accept PDF/DOCX/PPTX up to 100MB and 300 pages (recommended under 10); video is MP4 75KB–500MB, 3s–30min, 360–1920px, ±5% ratio tolerance. Google Performance Max has the most rigid asset contract (3–15 headlines at 30 chars, 1–5 long headlines at 90, 2–5 descriptions at 90, 1.91:1/1:1 images min 1, portrait 4:5 optional, 5120KB per image cap).

For a solo operator, API automation is realistic but tiered: Meta's default Limited tier is score-60/300s throttled and needs 500 successful calls with <15% errors to upgrade; Google Ads needs a Basic developer token (15,000 ops/day, ~5 business days); LinkedIn's Development Tier caps you at 5 ad accounts and requires 3-legged OAuth only; TikTok's docs are JS-rendered and could not be verified.

Compliance checking is entirely solvable free: sharp + ffprobe + Tesseract/PaddleOCR + a ΔE2000 brand-colour check covers aspect, size, codec, duration, safe-zone and text-coverage validation deterministically. The premium lane (Cloudinary g_auto, imgproxy Pro, Creatomate, Firefly Services) buys smart-crop quality and render throughput, not correctness. The material new legal fact: EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations became applicable on 2 August 2026 — twelve days ago.

**sharp (libvips binding)** — VERIFIED v0.35.3 (July 2026). Fit modes: cover (default), contain, fill, inside, outside. Position: top/right top/right/right bottom/bottom/left bottom/left/left top plus compass gravity. Two smart strategies: sharp.strategy.entropy ('focus on the region with the highest Shannon entropy') and sharp.strategy.attention ('focus on the region with the highest luminance frequency, colour saturation and presence of skin tones'). attention is your free content-aware crop for 1:1→9:16 and 4:5→1.91:1 derivations. Also has extend (letterbox padding with mirror/repeat) and trim (edge removal with threshold). Use .metadata() for width/height/format/size to drive the compliance validator.

**smartcrop.js / smartcrop-sharp** — VERIFIED: v2.0.5, ~13k stars, MIT. Pipeline: Laplace edge detection → skin-tone region detection → high-saturation region detection → weighted boosts (feed it face-detection boxes as boosts) → sliding-window crop candidates → rank to centre detail and avoid edge content. Server-side variants: smartcrop-sharp (libvips, fast) and smartcrop-gm (ImageMagick). smartcrop-cli exists as a reference implementation. Feeding it face boxes from a detector materially improves results on people-photos — that is the documented extension point.

**ffmpeg / ffprobe** — Concrete validator: ffprobe -v quiet -print_format json -show_format -show_streams in.mp4 gives you codec_name (assert h264), profile, pix_fmt (assert yuv420p for maximum compatibility), width/height, r_frame_rate, duration, bit_rate, and audio codec_name (assert aac) + bit_rate (assert ≥128000 for Meta). Compute aspect = width/height and assert within ±1% (Instagram), ±3% (Facebook), ±5% (LinkedIn). Always encode with -movflags +faststart. For safe-zone QA render a translucent overlay: drawbox at y=0 h=0.14*H, y=0.65*H h=0.35*H, x=0 w=0.06*W, and x=0.94*W — export a contact sheet for the human approval queue.

**ImageMagick** — Useful specifics: `identify -format '%w %h %[fx:w/h] %b'` for a one-line spec check; `compare -metric RMSE a.png b.png null:` to prove a resize did not drift; `montage` to build the human-approval contact sheet of all N placements from one master. Slower than libvips at scale — do not use it as the main resize engine.

**OpenCV (saliency + text detection)** — For static-to-video reframing, compute a per-frame saliency centroid, then temporally smooth it (a 1-euro filter or simple EMA over ~0.5s) to produce a virtual camera path — that smoothing step is what separates 'good' content-aware reframing from jittery garbage. Also detect shot boundaries first and reset the camera per shot rather than panning across cuts. Note: opencv-contrib includes some non-free/patented modules historically — verify your build's module set before shipping commercially.

**Tesseract OCR** — VERIFIED: major version 5 is current stable, Apache-2.0, LSTM engine since v4 (--oem 1), 100+ languages via traineddata. Turkish is available as `tur` traineddata from tessdata/tessdata_best. Key insight for your use case: you do NOT need accurate character recognition — you need bounding boxes. Use `tesseract img.png out tsv` and take the level-5 word boxes, union them, and compute (a) union area / total area for text coverage, and (b) whether any box intersects the safe-zone mask. Boxes are far more reliable than the transcribed text, so Tesseract is fine here even for Turkish diacritics.

**PaddleOCR** — VERIFIED: PaddleOCR 3.7.0 (June 2026), Apache-2.0, 100+ languages including 46 Latin-script languages (Turkish falls under Latin-script coverage but is not explicitly enumerated — test it). PP-OCRv6 is the latest detector/recogniser with tiny (1.5M params), small (7.7M) and medium (34.5M) tiers, claiming 5.2x CPU inference speedup over v5 and +4.6% detection / +5.1% recognition.
